LIBRARY OFFICE

1. Borrowing of Books

Borrowing of books refers to the process of charging library materials to legitimate students, faculty, or staff of Abuyog Community College. The circulation librarian or staff determines whether the book is for reserve or regular circulation. Reserved books may be used only within library premises and are not allowed for overnight borrowing. Circulation books may be borrowed overnight, while fiction books may be borrowed for seven (7) days using a validated Borrower’s Card.

Office/Unit:
Library Office

Classification:
Standard

Type of Transaction:
G2C – Government to Citizen
G2G – Government to Government

Who May Avail:
Students, Faculty, and Staff of Abuyog Community College

Checklist of Requirements and Where to Secure:
- Validated ACC Borrower’s Card – ACC Library (Circulation Unit)
- Validated ACC ID – ACC Registrar

Client Steps and Agency Actions:
Client Step 1:
Write the date, full name, course, year level, and signature on the book card.
Agency Action:
The librarian compares the book information with the book card, Borrower’s Card, and the actual book.
Fee: None
Processing Time: 2 minutes per book
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Client Step 2:
Present the book(s) and Borrower’s Card to the librarian or staff on duty.
Agency Action:
Write the accession number and due date on the Borrower’s Card and release the borrowed book(s).
Fee: None
Processing Time: 2 minutes per book
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Total Processing Time:
4 minutes per book

2. Borrowed Book Check-in

Borrowed Book Check-in is the process of returning borrowed library materials. The circulation librarian or staff checks whether the book is overdue or damaged. Applicable fines are imposed based on the Library Handbook. Once returned on or before the due date, the borrower’s obligation ends and records are updated.

Office/Unit:
Library Office

Classification:
Standard

Type of Transaction:
G2C – Government to Citizen
G2G – Government to Government

Who May Avail:
Students, Faculty, and Staff of Abuyog Community College

Checklist of Requirements and Where to Secure:
- Validated ACC Borrower’s Card – ACC Library (Circulation Unit)
- Validated ACC ID – ACC Registrar

Client Steps and Agency Actions:
Client Step 1:
Present the borrowed book(s) to the librarian or staff on duty.
Agency Action:
Receive the book(s) and verify call number and accession number against records.
Fee: None
Processing Time: 1 minute and 30 seconds per book
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Agency Action:
Inspect returned materials and apply penalties for overdue or damaged items if applicable.
Fee: PHP 50.00 per day per book (excluding weekends and holidays)
Processing Time: 2 minutes
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Agency Action:
Record the return date on the Borrower’s Card and return it to the owner.
Fee: None
Processing Time: 1 minute
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Total Processing Time:
4 minutes and 30 seconds per book

--------------------------------------------------

3. Issuance of Borrower’s Card

The issuance of a Borrower’s Card is required to borrow books from the ACC Library. This card contains the borrower’s personal information and is issued only to legitimate students, faculty, or staff.

Office/Unit:
Library Office

Classification:
Standard

Type of Transaction:
G2C – Government to Citizen
G2G – Government to Government

Who May Avail:
Students, Faculty, and Staff of Abuyog Community College

Checklist of Requirements and Where to Secure:
- Validated ACC ID – ACC Registrar
- 1x1 ID Picture – Provided by applicant

Client Steps and Agency Actions:
Client Step 1:
Present ACC ID and 1x1 picture.
Agency Action:
Verify and confirm accuracy of information.
Fee: None
Processing Time: 1 minute
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Client Step 2:
Fill out the Borrower’s Card application form.
Agency Action:
Verify details, paste ID picture, validate, and release Borrower’s Card.
Fee: PHP 20.00 per card
Processing Time: 3 minutes
Responsible Person: Librarian/Staff

Total Processing Time:
4 minutes

4. Signing of Clearance

Clearance signing is conducted at the ACC Library Circulation Unit. The librarian verifies whether the client has outstanding library obligations before signing the clearance.

Office/Unit:
Library Office

Classification:
Standard

Type of Transaction:
G2C – Government to Citizen
G2G – Government to Government

Who May Avail:
Students, Faculty, and Staff of Abuyog Community College
