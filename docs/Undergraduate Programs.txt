INSTITUTION: ABUYOG COMMUNITY COLLEGE

=====================================
COLLEGE OF ARTS, SCIENCE, AND EDUCATION (CASE)
=====================================

PROGRAMS:
- Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd)
  Majors:
  - Mathematics
  - Science
  - English
  - Social Studies
- Bachelor of Elementary Education (BEEd)

VISION:
An innovative and responsive college that prepares educators with strong teaching, research, and professional skills for societal development.

MISSION:
To produce professional educators who demonstrate mastery in teaching and can address educational needs at local, regional, and national levels through effective pedagogy, research, and extension services.

GOALS:
- Offer 4-year teacher education programs (BEEd and BSEd).
- Provide BSEd specializations: English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies.
- Align teaching and research with community and industry needs.
- Adapt to curriculum changes and educational trends.
- Ensure quality education with updated and relevant course content.
- Develop students’ teaching skills, classroom management, and assessment abilities.
- Prepare students for pre-service training and professional practice.
- Increase LET passers and produce top-performing graduates.

=====================================
COLLEGE OF MANAGEMENT AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP (CME)
=====================================

PROGRAMS:
- Bachelor of Science in Entrepreneurship (BSEntrep)
- Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management (BSHM)

VISION:
To be a leading business college known for excellence in entrepreneurship and hospitality education, producing skilled graduates and contributing to local industry.

MISSION:
To provide accessible, high-quality education that promotes integrity and academic excellence, preparing students for professional careers in a global environment.

GOALS:
- Equip students with skills for the modern business world.
- Develop leadership and teamwork abilities.
- Train students in research for community development.

=====================================
COLLEGE OF CRIMINAL JUSTICE EDUCATION (CCJE)
=====================================

PROGRAM:
- Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSCrim)

VISION:
To be a leading institution producing graduates who address crime-related issues and meet global standards in criminal justice.

MISSION:
To develop competent and ethical graduates who provide effective services in public safety and law enforcement.

=====================================
COLLEGE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY EDUCATION (CITE)
=====================================

PROGRAM:
- Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)

VISION:
To be a center of excellence in IT education and research, producing innovative professionals and collaborating with industry and government.

MISSION:
- Provide high-quality IT education.
- Develop future IT leaders.
- Support teaching, research, and industry collaboration.

GOALS:
- Achieve excellence in IT education.
- Provide strong IT knowledge for local and global impact.
- Build partnerships with institutions for research and development.
