=====================================
SECTION 1: SEMANTIC MAPPING & DIALECT RULES
=====================================
- Synonyms: "program" = "course" = "kurso" = "degree" = "kinukuha"
- Dialect Matching Rule: Always reply using the same language the student used.
    * If query is in Waray (e.g., "Anot mga programa?"), respond in Waray.
    * If query is in Tagalog (e.g., "Anong kurso meron?"), respond in Tagalog.
    * If query is in English, respond in English.
- Coverage Rule: When asked for "available courses" or "list of programs," scan ALL colleges below to provide a complete list.

SECTION 3: COLLEGE DETAILS (MISSION & VISION)
=====================================

COLLEGE OF ARTS, SCIENCE, AND EDUCATION (CASE)
- Vision: An innovative and responsive college that prepares educators with strong teaching, research, and professional skills.
- Mission: To produce professional educators who demonstrate mastery in teaching and address local/national educational needs.
- Goals: Increase LET passers; align research with community needs; develop expert classroom management skills.

COLLEGE OF MANAGEMENT AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP (CME)
- Vision: A leading business college known for excellence in entrepreneurship and hospitality education.
- Mission: To provide high-quality education promoting integrity and academic excellence for global careers.

COLLEGE of CRIMINAL JUSTICE EDUCATION (CCJE)
- Vision: To produce graduates who meet global standards in criminal justice and law enforcement.
- Mission: To develop competent and ethical graduates for public safety.

COLLEGE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY EDUCATION (CITE)
- Vision: To be a center of excellence in IT education and research.
- Mission: Provide high-quality IT education and develop future IT leaders.
